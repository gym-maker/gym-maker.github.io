    Frequently asked questions

    When is the last day to ship for Christmas 2026?

    It depends on the carrier, service, and destination. For the contiguous U.S., USPS recommends Thursday, December 17 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Friday, December 18 for Priority Mail, and Saturday, December 19 for Priority Mail Express, for expected delivery before December 25. UPS and FedEx last-day tables are for arrival by December 24, because they are closed on Christmas Day.

    Why aren’t the UPS and FedEx dates for December 25 delivery?

    Both carriers say they are closed on December 25, with no pickup or delivery. Their official 2026 tables list last days to ship for scheduled arrival by December 24. This page does not relabel those dates as Christmas Day delivery.

    Are these dates a delivery guarantee?

    No. USPS calls them recommended send-by dates; actual delivery may vary with origin, destination, and acceptance time. UPS notes that certain destinations may need an earlier ship date. FedEx Ground and Home Delivery last days depend on the transit time for your ZIP Codes. Dates can change — check the official pages before you ship.

    What if there is no single published date?

    This page shows “Not a single published date” (or “Not published yet” if a carrier had no 2026 table) and links to the official lookup. It never fills in a date from a previous year.
