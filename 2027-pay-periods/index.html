Federal Reserve Banks vs. OPM office closings in 2027

Payday settlement follows when Federal Reserve Banks are closed, not when federal employees have a day off. On a Saturday holiday, Fed Banks stay open the Friday before; the Board of Governors is closed that Friday. Most federal employees, under OPM rules, observe that Friday off.

FAQ

How many biweekly paychecks are there in 2027?

Most biweekly cycles have 26 paydays. 2027 starts and ends on a Friday, so it has 53 Fridays. A Friday cycle whose first 2027 payday is January 1 therefore has 27 scheduled Fridays, ending December 31. January 1 is New Year’s Day and Federal Reserve Banks are closed, so if your employer pays the previous business day, that check is dated Thursday, December 31, 2026, and 26 checks are paid in 2027. December 31, 2027 does not need to move: Fed Banks are open that Friday.

Which months have three biweekly paychecks in 2027?

Friday cycle starting January 1: scheduled three-paycheck months are January, July, and December. After moving January 1 into 2026, the paid three-paycheck months are July (2, 16, 30) and December (3, 17, 31). Friday cycle starting January 8: April (2, 16, 30) and October (1, 15, 29).

Do Juneteenth, Christmas Eve, or New Year’s Eve paydays move in 2027?

Not for bank settlement. Federal Reserve Banks stay open Friday June 18, Friday December 24, and Friday December 31. Those Fridays are OPM observed holidays for most federal employees, but they are not Fed Bank closures. A payday due Friday January 1 does move, to Thursday December 31, 2026.

Is this the official federal employee or OPM pay calendar?

No. Federal employees should use the official calendar from their payroll provider (NFC, GSA, DFAS, or their agency). This is a planning calendar for common private-employer cycles. Your employer’s dates win, and some employers do not move holiday paydays.
