FAQ

Does this store names or send emails?

No. The draw runs in your browser. Names never leave your device, and there is no sign-up or email sending.

Are the private reveal links secure?

No. Each assignment is only lightly hidden in the link (the part after #). Anyone who has the link, or who can see your browser history, can read it. Do not post the links in a shared chat if you want them to stay private.

Can couples be excluded from drawing each other?

Yes. Add a couple as an exclusion and they will not be assigned to each other. A group of three with one couple cannot produce a valid draw, because both partners would have to give to the same third person. You need at least four people for a couple exclusion to work.

What do the printable cards look like?

Each card has the recipient’s name on the outside and who they are buying for below a fold line. Print them and fold so only the outer name shows until the card is opened.
