FAQ

How many federal holidays are there in 2027?

11. Three land on a weekend and move: Juneteenth (Sat Jun 19 → Fri Jun 18), Independence Day (Sun Jul 4 → Mon Jul 5) and Christmas (Sat Dec 25 → Fri Dec 24).

When is Thanksgiving 2027?

Thursday, November 25, 2027. Take Friday Nov 26 off and you get a 4-day weekend.

Is December 31, 2027 a day off?

For most federal employees, yes. New Year's Day 2028 is a Saturday, so it's observed on Friday, December 31, 2027 (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)). OPM lists it with the 2028 holidays, so it's marked separately here.

How many working days are in 2027?

2027 has 261 weekdays. Minus the 11 federal holidays observed on weekdays, that's 250 working days (249 if you also take Dec 31 off).

Can I print it?

Yes. Hit "Print 2027 calendar" and the page switches to a 12-month, one-page layout. Save as PDF from the print dialog if you want a file.
